DiskMap 0.2.0 adds standard Light and Dark themes alongside the Inactive Node theme.

## Download

Download **DiskMap-0.2.0-macOS-universal.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **DiskMap.app** into your Applications folder, replacing the old version. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 12 or later.

DiskMap isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time you open it:

1. Open DiskMap. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about DiskMap, and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. After that, DiskMap opens normally.

## What's new

- An **Appearance** menu in the toolbar with four choices: **Inactive Node** (the original theme, still the default), **Light**, **Dark**, and **Match system**, which follows your Mac between light and dark mode.
- Light and Dark use macOS's system greys, blue for selection and links, the system fonts, and the standard Mac colours for file types on the map.
- DiskMap remembers your choice the next time you open it.

## Known limitations

- The window's title bar follows macOS's appearance rather than DiskMap's theme, so picking Light while your Mac is in dark mode gives a dark title bar.
- The Intel version is built and included but hasn't been tested on an Intel Mac yet.
- Because the app isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate, macOS may ask you to turn Full Disk Access on again after you install this version.
